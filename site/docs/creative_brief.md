# Abdelrhman Shoman — Restart Source of Truth

## Brand

Abdelrhman Shoman is a **systems builder** working across operations, automation, AI, data, research, and computational workflows. Healthcare and biomedical research are examples of domain experience, not the central identity. The visitor should leave thinking: “He knows how to build systems,” not merely “He knows many tools.”

## Central idea

Different problems. Same approach.

The recurring method is: **Problem → Understand → Map → Design → Build → Automate → Measure → Improve**.

## Experience principle

The website should feel like **a system you can explore**, not a CV inside a 3D website. The room is the interface; objects are navigation; the camera is scroll; the laptop is technology; the notebook is thinking; the whiteboard is methodology; the monitor is work; the network is career; the window is future.

## Visual direction

Create an original Blender-cinematic personal workspace: tasteful, calm, warm physical materials, minimal editorial overlays, restrained muted blue or green technical accent, and no cyberpunk neon, excessive gradients, glowing-everything treatment, or corporate office styling. Use warm sunlight, cool laptop/monitor light, natural wood, warm grey, beige, off-white, and near-black.

## Story states

1. **INTRO** — black initialization, then a wide room shot with closed laptop, off monitor, closed notebook, scattered whiteboard notes, and `I BUILD SYSTEMS.`
2. **PROBLEM** — move toward the desk; papers and cables introduce complexity.
3. **CHAOS** — laptop activates with messy email/spreadsheet/message/manual-update flow.
4. **METHOD** — enter the laptop and reveal the ordered system sequence.
5. **NOTEBOOK** — move to thinking and methodology pages.
6. **WHITEBOARD** — move from messy process map to structured flow.
7. **EXPERIENCE** — reveal DECI/Almentor, iSkyTech, education/community, and growth systems as grounded case studies.
8. **SYSTEMS LAB** — monitor becomes a systems map with Operations, Automation, AI, Data, and Research nodes.
9. **INTEGRATION** — different problems collapse into the same approach; the room becomes organized and connected.
10. **ABOUT / FUTURE** — pharmacy → research → computation → operations → automation → AI → systems building, then move toward the city-facing window.
11. **CONTACT / FINAL** — laptop asks `HAVE A COMPLEX PROBLEM?` and ends outside the window with `SYSTEM READY`.

## Content rules

Use only verified claims. Do not fabricate metrics, clients, revenue, user counts, impact numbers, or technical capabilities. Case studies should use: Problem, Context, My Role, System, Design, Technology, Result, Lesson, and Next Iteration. If a result is not verified, use qualitative language.

## Interaction rules

Use a central story state. Scroll controls camera, object movement, opacity, scale, screen content, lighting, and transitions. Interactive objects use labels such as `EXPLORE →`, `OPEN SYSTEM →`, `READ →`, `ENTER →`, `UNDERSTAND →`, and `NEXT →`. Audio remains optional and never forced. Mobile preserves the concept with reduced camera travel, simplified objects, and 2D system cards when needed.

## Technical direction

React + Vite + Three.js, with optimized browser-safe geometry, limited real-time lights, adaptive pixel ratio, and reduced effects on mobile. Preserve return-to-scene behavior when opening case studies.
