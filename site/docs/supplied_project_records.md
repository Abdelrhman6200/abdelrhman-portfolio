## Featured systems

### Session Link Automation System

**Domain:** Operational automation. **Core flow:** Microsoft Teams session creation → naming convention → link distribution → attendance connection. **What it demonstrates:** Power Automate workflow design, process optimization, and high-volume execution. **Reported outcome:** A process described as taking days was reduced to hours while reducing human error; the brief describes a scale of thousands of sessions per month.

### Smart Excel Validation Framework

**Domain:** Data quality and operational control. **Core flow:** Large student dataset → custom Excel functions and conditional rules → exception flags → staff review. **What it demonstrates:** Data validation, automation, and large-scale data management across more than 40,000 students. **Reported outcomes:** 90% reduction in errors and 70% faster review.

### Certification & Project Dashboard

**Domain:** Assessment automation. **Core flow:** Project intake → grading process → result tracking → certification status → certificate issuance. **What it demonstrates:** Dashboard design, assessment systems, automation, and scale across more than 5,000 student projects. **Reported outcome:** Certificates could be issued within 24 hours while maintaining accurate and fair evaluation.

### AI Operational Agent

**Domain:** AI knowledge operations. **Core flow:** Employee question → request understanding → relevant SOP or organizational knowledge retrieval → operational answer. **What it demonstrates:** AI agents, knowledge management, retrieval workflows, and internal automation. **Reported outcome:** Significantly reduced response time.

### AI-Assisted Feedback Management Application

**Domain:** Human-AI collaboration. **Core flow:** Feedback context → structured draft → template and tone suggestions → human review → final communication. **What it demonstrates:** AI-assisted workflow design and communication automation. **Reported outcome:** 60% faster feedback creation.

## Operational infrastructure

### Session Coding & LMS Integration System

Session identification encoded governorate, area, and vendor attributes, then integrated the logic into Docebo. The system demonstrates data architecture, LMS integration, filtering, and operational traceability. **Reported outcome:** Faster filtering and clearer session traceability.

### Instructor & Coordinator Management Database

Centralized operational data for instructors, coordinators, and supervisors, including onboarding, assigned sessions, progress, performance, quality scores, and evaluations. The system demonstrates database design, workforce management, and operational visibility. **Reported outcome:** Full operational visibility.

### Centralized Department Synchronization

Interconnected sheets and dashboards connected operations, marketing, sales, product, and HR so updates could travel across departments. The system demonstrates cross-functional data synchronization and shared operational intelligence. **Reported outcome:** Fewer bottlenecks caused by disconnected information.

### SOP Library & Operational Policy Framework
